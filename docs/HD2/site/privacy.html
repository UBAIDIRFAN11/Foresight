Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

Your financial records

Foresight stores your manually entered financial profile, savings goals, saved purchase decisions and reminder records locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. These records remain until you delete them. Foresight has no user accounts, banking connection or app-operated cloud synchronisation. The developer does not receive your financial records.

Preferences and backups

The app stores reminder preferences and a notification-cleanup retry flag in its own UserDefaults. iOS controls device backups, which may include local app data depending on your settings. Foresight does not provide a backup service. Erase All Data does not remove existing device or iCloud backups; manage those through Apple's settings.

Exchange-rate requests

When a calculation needs a foreign-currency exchange rate, Foresight sends the two currency codes to api.frankfurter.dev over HTTPS. It does not include your savings, income, expenses, goal names or purchase amounts. Same-currency calculations do not need this request. The internet service and network infrastructure process connection metadata to deliver it. Frankfurter's published policy states that its API does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses or request URLs. See https://frankfurter.dev/ for its current policy.

Optional on-device explanations

On supported devices, Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework temporarily processes selected financial context to explain results already calculated by Foresight. Foresight does not send these prompts or explanations to the developer or a cloud AI service, and does not save the generated explanation to its financial database. AI explanations can be incorrect and are not professional financial advice.

Local reminders

Foresight asks for notification permission when you enable a reminder. iOS schedules notifications locally; there is no push-notification server. A notification title can include your goal or purchase name, so choose names suitable for your device's notification previews. You can pause or disable reminders in Foresight and control permission and preview visibility in iOS Settings. Erasing app data does not revoke the system permission.

No tracking or analytics

Foresight includes no advertising, tracking SDK, analytics SDK or third-party sign-in. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or health data.

Erase All Data

Settings > Privacy & Data > Erase All Data asks for confirmation before removing your profile, all goals, saved history and reminder records. It also resets reminder preferences and removes this app's pending and delivered notifications. This cannot be undone in the app. If saving the deletion fails, no deletion is committed. If notification cleanup is incomplete after records were erased, those records stay erased; the app explains the partial result, pauses scheduling and offers cleanup retry, including on the next launch. No additional financial records are deleted by a cleanup retry. The same privacy policy is available before onboarding. You can also remove local app data by deleting the app through iOS.
