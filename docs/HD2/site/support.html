Where is my data?

Financial records are stored locally. There is no account or app cloud synchronisation.

Does it work offline?

Same-currency planning works offline. A foreign-currency calculation needs an internet connection for the rate.

Why is AI unavailable?

Explanations require a supported Apple Intelligence device, model availability and a supported language. Calculations work without AI.

How can I erase my data?

Open Settings, Privacy & Data, then Erase All Data. Read the confirmation carefully. Existing OS backups are managed separately.

Local data will not open

Check free device storage and retry. Restart the app or device if needed. Contact support before reinstalling because reinstalling may delete local records.
